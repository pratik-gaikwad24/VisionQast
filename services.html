FAQ

Questions, Answered

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in Google's search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures your content so it is picked as the direct answer in featured snippets, “People Also Ask” and voice assistants. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) makes your brand visible and cited inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot.

Do I need Meta Ads, Google Ads or LinkedIn Ads?

It depends on your buyer. Google Ads captures people already searching for what you sell. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) builds demand and works well for consumer brands, local businesses and retargeting. LinkedIn Ads reach decision-makers by job title, industry and company size, which suits B2B. We recommend the right mix after a free audit.

Is ad spend included in your fees?

No. Ad budgets are paid directly to Meta, Google or LinkedIn from your own ad account, so you keep full ownership and transparency. Our fee covers strategy, creatives, setup, management and reporting.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes and local SEO improvements can show movement within 4–8 weeks. Competitive keyword rankings typically take 3–6 months of consistent work. We share monthly ranking, traffic and lead reports so progress is always visible.

Can you handle my website and marketing together?

Yes, and that's where we shine. Because we build the website and run the marketing, landing pages, tracking, speed and SEO are set up correctly from day one, and every rupee of ad spend lands on a page designed to convert.
