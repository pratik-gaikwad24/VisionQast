How much does a website or marketing campaign cost?

Every business is different, so we don't sell one-size packages. After a free call we share a fixed written quote for build projects, or a flat monthly fee for marketing, with no hidden charges. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta, Google or LinkedIn from your own account.

How long does it take to build a website?

A business website typically takes 2–4 weeks, an e-commerce store 4–6 weeks, and custom software or apps 6–12 weeks depending on scope. You get a clear timeline with milestones before we start.

Do I own my website, ad accounts and data?

Yes. Your domain, website code, ad accounts, analytics and content belong to you. We work inside accounts in your name, so you're never locked in.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Yes. We're based in Pune and work with clients across India and abroad over video calls, WhatsApp and email.

How will I know if marketing is working?

You get a live dashboard plus a monthly report covering leads, cost per lead, ROAS, rankings, traffic and AI-search visibility, along with a call to plan the next month.
